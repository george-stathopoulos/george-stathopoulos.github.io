Approach to AI

AI used carefully, and for people

I build AI into support and product workflows every day, so I've seen what it does well and where it goes wrong. My rule is simple: AI should never replace people. It should give them back time for the conversations that matter.

“The best use of AI is not fewer people. It's people with more time for each other.”

How I approach every AI project

  • People stay in charge

    AI drafts, sorts and suggests. People decide, especially when a customer is upset, money is involved or the answer isn't obvious.

  • Automate the repetitive, not the relationship

    Let automation answer the same question for the hundredth time, so the team can give real attention to the conversations that need a human.

  • Privacy by default

    Customer data shouldn't leave your hands without a reason. It's why I built Gatehouse to strip personal data before it ever reaches an AI provider.

  • Honest about it

    People deserve to know when they're talking to a machine, and they should always be able to reach a person.

  • Measure, then trust

    Every AI workflow has to earn its place with evidence: quality, customer satisfaction and cost, reviewed regularly rather than assumed. If it doesn't make the experience better for customers and the team, it goes.
